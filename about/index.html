About

Last updated 23 Oct 2026

Luis Gimenez

Data scientist · forecasting, causal inference and LLM applications

Kraków, Poland · lgimenezuso@gmail.com · https://www.linkedin.com/in/luis-gimenez-uso · https://github.com/luisgius

I'm a data scientist at Uber in Kraków. I work on driver supply forecasting, and on turning business questions into hypotheses that causal inference and experimental design can test.

Before Uber I was a junior data scientist at Buynomics in Cologne, building revenue prediction and clustering models for a Tier-1 CPG client. Before that I interned at Porsche AG in Stuttgart, automating test-bench data collection and building Power BI dashboards, and at Capgemini in Valencia as an ERP consultant. I studied computer science and business administration as a dual degree at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia.

I also like to write about philosophy.

2023 now

  1. 2026 – now

    Data Scientist (current role)

    Uber, Kraków, Poland

    Forecasting driver supply, and using causal inference and experimental design to guide supply-side operational decisions.

    • Built and deployed an ML forecasting system for driver supply (LightGBM quantile regression with conformal prediction intervals) at city × product × week granularity, outperforming the incumbent manual forecasting process.
    • Formulated business questions as testable hypotheses, applying causal inference and experimental design to estimate effects and guide supply-side operational decisions.
    • Engineered features and cleaned large-scale datasets (Python, SQL over Presto and Spark), and shipped model overrides through a shadow-to-live rollout, validating performance against production before launch.
    • Visualized and reported findings to stakeholders, delivering forecast reliability analyses and full project documentation for technical and business audiences.

    Stack: Python, SQL, LightGBM, Presto, Spark

  2. 2025 – 2026

    Junior Data Scientist

    Buynomics, Cologne, Germany

    Revenue prediction and clustering models for a Tier-1 CPG client, and the data pipelines that feed the Buynomics ML platform.

    • Designed Python ETL workflows to connect raw country-level data to the Buynomics ML platform, improving data reliability and model training speed.
